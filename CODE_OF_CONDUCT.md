# Code of Conduct

ProFSea is developed in the open, and we want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull
requests, discussions or any other project space) to have a welcoming, respectful and
harassment-free experience, whatever their background, identity or level of experience.

We expect participants to:

- be kind and respectful, and assume good intent;
- give and accept constructive feedback gracefully;
- focus on what is best for the project and its community.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and other unwelcoming behaviour are not
tolerated. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or
permanently exclude anyone whose behaviour breaks this code.

To report a concern, contact the maintainers. Reports will
be handled promptly and in confidence.

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
